# Candidate Plans — index

Ten distinct product directions explored for the Claude Code Build Day, generated **in parallel by
Multica agents** (each as its own branch → PR into `main`). All ten are landed below.

**Outcome:** Plans **01** and **03** were chosen and combined into the build-day project —
**[`../solution/`](../solution/)**, a general decision outcome simulator. The other eight remain as
documented alternatives.

| # | Product | One-liner | Doc | Status |
|---|---------|-----------|-----|--------|
| 01 | **Lynsea** | A living world of the people/forces around a decision, fast-forwarded under each option, futures shown side-by-side | [01-life-sim-sandbox.md](01-life-sim-sandbox.md) | ✅ **chosen → [solution/](../solution/)** |
| 02 | Coaching Copilot | Runs named, citable CBT protocols; assigns/tracks homework; adapts each session — a therapist who never forgets and shows their work | [02-coaching-copilot.md](02-coaching-copilot.md) | ✅ landed |
| 03 | **Decision Twin** | A digital twin of *you* that lives out each branch of a fork-in-the-road decision for 5 simulated years | [03-decision-twin.md](03-decision-twin.md) | ✅ **chosen → [solution/](../solution/)** |
| 04 | Praxis | A multi-agent "standardized patient" so counselors can practice a real session and get rubric-scored, evidence-cited feedback | [04-counselor-trainer.md](04-counselor-trainer.md) | ✅ landed |
| 05 | Keel | A digital twin of your willpower that simulates tomorrow's you against science-based nudges and sends only the best one | [05-habit-engine.md](05-habit-engine.md) | ✅ landed |
| 06 | Health Persona | A queryable digital twin of your body from wearable/lab/journal data — "what if I slept one more hour?" | [06-health-persona.md](06-health-persona.md) | ✅ landed |
| 07 | CoachArena | A benchmark grading coaching LLMs on whether simulated users actually *change their life* over 30 days | [07-coach-benchmark.md](07-coach-benchmark.md) | ✅ landed |
| 08 | Threshold | A safe mental-health front door — a panel that splits "listen" from "decide" so no single model can mishandle a crisis | [08-safety-triage.md](08-safety-triage.md) | ✅ landed |
| 09 | RehearseRoom | A household simulator to rehearse a dreaded conversation against a faithful twin of the other person | [09-relationship-sim.md](09-relationship-sim.md) | ✅ landed |
| 10 | Coach Studio | A no-code studio where Claude Code compiles dragged "coaching blocks" into a runnable multi-agent coach | [10-coach-studio.md](10-coach-studio.md) | ✅ landed |

Each plan follows [`../docs/PLAN_TEMPLATE.md`](../docs/PLAN_TEMPLATE.md) and aligns to
[`../docs/HACKATHON_BRIEF.md`](../docs/HACKATHON_BRIEF.md), and ends with a self-score against the
three judging criteria (technical innovation · implementation quality · potential impact).
