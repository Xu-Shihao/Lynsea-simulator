03技术 · How it works
A controlled experiment on your future — not a guess.
🎲
Fixed-seed paired counterfactualM-b
Branches differ only by the decision; shared random events are byte-identical. Reproducible seed hash (NFR-01) — re-runnable, testable.
🧠
Digital-twin personasM-c
You + your social circle as belief-driven twins (UserHarness, arXiv:2605.27721). State-forking keeps each persona consistent across both branches.
⚖️
5-dim value-weighted scoringM-d
Economic · career · relationships · mental · autonomy, weighted to your priorities. "Better" means better for you.
📊
Credibility card
Data · causal · plausibility scored every run. Always probabilistic, always directional — a simulation, never a prophecy.
